1985 — THE BEGINNING
A LEGACY OF LOVE

Every heirloom begins somewhere.

In 1985, our family's jewellery journey began with a belief that the most meaningful pieces are never just ornaments. They carry the celebrations, relationships and memories that make us who we are.

For years, the business remained a family endeavour, shaped by care, trust and a love for jewellery that feels personal.

Then came a new ambition: to share that spirit beyond our own community. ZEVORA's global chapter brings our family story together with design inspirations from around the world — and invites you to create something uniquely yours.
